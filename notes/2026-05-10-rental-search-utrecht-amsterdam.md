## Reality check on budget

"All-in €1,500" ≈ **kale huur €1,150–1,300** after typical €150 service + €150–200 utilities. That puts you at the top of the *middenhuur* (mid-rent) regulated segment, which is good news:

- Since 1 July 2024, the Dutch points system (WWS) caps rent up to ~€1,158/month kale, and a middenhuur band runs above that depending on points and year built. Landlords can't legally exceed the points-based max. Source: [Rijksoverheid – Wet betaalbare huur](https://www.rijksoverheid.nl/onderwerpen/woningmarkt/wet-betaalbare-huur).
- Practical effect: well-located 1-beds in Almere, Amersfoort outskirts, Lelystad, Nijkerk are realistic. Hilversum/Bussum/central Utrecht/central Amsterdam are not.
- Also: most "free sector" listings on Pararius now exceed your budget. The cheap stock is on **institutional landlord portals** (see below) and regional Facebook groups — not the big aggregators.

### Recommendation

1. **Amersfoort** — primary target. Short car commute *now*, decent train *later*. Stock at €1,150–1,300 kale exists in Liendert, Schothorst, Kruiskamp.
2. **Almere (Poort or Muziekwijk)** — backup. Better budget headroom and her future commute, but you eat 50 min in traffic on A27 daily. Reconsider if you go hybrid 2–3 days/week.
3. **Nijkerk** — wildcard if Amersfoort listings are dry. Quieter, shorter your-commute, but her future train doubles vs Amersfoort.

## Workflow for the 1–2 month timeline

Dutch rental market in 2026 is still landlord-favored. Listings at €1,200–1,300 in nice areas go in **24–72 hours**. To compete:

1. **Daily monitoring**, not weekly. Set Pararius + Funda alerts (free). Institutional sites usually require manual refresh.
2. **Pre-build the application packet**:
   - 3 most recent payslips (yours)
   - Employer statement (werkgeversverklaring)
   - Copy of ID
   - Bank statement showing salary deposits
   - Letter explaining her situation (no income yet, but actively job-hunting in Amsterdam)
3. **Income rule**: most landlords require 3.5–4× monthly rent in gross income. For €1,500 rent → €5,250–6,000 gross/mo. If you alone clear that, fine. If not, you'll need a guarantor or savings statement.
4. **Viewing strategy**: respond within an hour, accept first viewing slot, bring the packet to the viewing. People who decide on the spot win.
